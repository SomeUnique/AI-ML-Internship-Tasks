TITLE: Pakistan's AI Ecosystem in 2026: Startups, Software, and More
PUBLISHER: Digital Pakistan
DATE: 2026
URL: https://digitalpakistan.pk/pakistan-ai-ecosystem
---
This industry overview describes Pakistan's SME sector as an "untapped
market" for AI adoption, noting that most small businesses operate on tight
margins with manual, fragmented workflows. It characterizes current SME AI
usage as unglamorous but practical: tier-one customer service chatbots,
accounting software with built-in anomaly detection, and demand forecasting
tools for retail and distribution businesses. The piece highlights a
behavioral shift as the most important trend — business owners who were
skeptical of software just a few years ago are now actively requesting
automation, which the author frames as the foundation for further AI
adoption. Separately, the article notes that Pakistani AI/SaaS startups
raised over $74 million in funding in 2025, with fintech, healthtech, AI,
and B2B SaaS attracting the most investor interest, reflecting a recovery
in the local funding environment after a post-2021 slowdown.
