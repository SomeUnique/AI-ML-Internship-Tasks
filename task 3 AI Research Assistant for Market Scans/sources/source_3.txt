TITLE: AI-powered export platforms unlock global markets for Pakistan's SMEs
PUBLISHER: INP-WealthPk (news)
DATE: June 16, 2026
URL: https://www.inp.net.pk/article-detail/inp-wealthpk/...
---
This news report focuses specifically on export-oriented AI adoption among
Pakistani SMEs, which according to SMEDA estimates contribute nearly 40% of
GDP and about 30% of the country's exports. It describes a partnership
announced in May 2026 between Alibaba.com and Pakistani stakeholders to
provide AI and digital-skills training to 10,000 SMEs and onboard at least
2,000 Pakistani businesses onto Alibaba's global marketplace, connecting
them to over 50 million international B2B buyers. The article links this to
broader digital-economy growth: Pakistan's IT and IT-enabled services
exports reached approximately $3.8 billion between July 2025 and April 2026,
a 21% year-on-year increase per Trade Development Authority of Pakistan
(TDAP) data. Industry sources quoted in the piece say AI-driven trade
platforms — automated product listings, multilingual communication,
buyer-seller matching, demand forecasting — are lowering entry barriers for
smaller exporters who previously couldn't afford overseas sales networks.
