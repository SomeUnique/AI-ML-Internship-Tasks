TITLE: AI Adoption in SMEs and Its Economic Impact on Pakistan's Development
PUBLISHER: ResearchGate (academic paper)
DATE: 2025 (approx.)
URL: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/395959783
---
This academic study surveyed 368 small and medium enterprises (SMEs) across
multiple industries in Pakistan to examine AI adoption patterns, investment
levels, perceived benefits, and barriers. The research found that nearly
two-thirds of surveyed SMEs have already adopted some form of AI, with the
heaviest usage concentrated in marketing, sales, and customer service
functions. Adoption in HR and product development remains comparatively
limited. The primary motivations driving adoption were efficiency gains,
cost reduction, and improved decision-making. However, the study identified
persistent barriers: high implementation costs, shortages of skilled staff,
limited awareness of available tools, and general resistance to change
within organizations. The authors argue that AI adoption in SMEs carries
macroeconomic significance for Pakistan, since SMEs are a major driver of
the country's economic activity, and that broader AI integration could
meaningfully boost national productivity if these barriers are addressed.
